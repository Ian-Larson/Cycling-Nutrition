## Design Context

### Users
Trained cyclists using the app before a ride, often on mobile, to build a fast and reliable fueling plan without doing nutrition math by hand. They need to confirm kit readiness, set ride demands, and leave with instructions they can follow on the bike.

### Brand Personality
Precise, calm, focused. The product should feel dependable and clear under time pressure. Copy should be brief, concrete, and useful to cyclists who want numbers and actions without marketing language.

### Aesthetic Direction
Quiet light-mode utility. A restrained palette anchored by `#547597`, `#C9C6C5`, and `#EBEBEB`, moderate contrast, minimal decoration, and compact typography that supports scanning. Avoid oversized headlines, dramatic hero sections, ornamental gradients, and copy that restates visible information.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize task completion: show the numbers and actions cyclists need first.
2. Keep copy tight: no duplicated explanations, no slogans, no filler.
3. Use one calm accent color and let neutrals carry the layout.
4. Build hierarchy through spacing and weight, not oversized type.
5. Make every control easy to scan on mobile and desktop.
