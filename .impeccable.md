## Design Context

### Users
Trained cyclists using the app before a ride, often on mobile, to build a fast and reliable fueling plan without doing nutrition math by hand. They need to confirm kit readiness, set ride demands, and leave with instructions they can follow on the bike.

### Brand Personality
Confident, athletic, precise. The product should feel race-day ready rather than clinical or generic. Copy should stay concise and performance-oriented while the interface evokes momentum, preparedness, and trust.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light-mode editorial athletics. Warm paper-tinted neutrals, a signal orange primary accent, condensed display typography for hierarchy, and structured layouts that feel intentional instead of SaaS-generic. Avoid blue-on-white dashboard styling, purple gradients, glassmorphism, and interchangeable rounded-card patterns.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with followability: plans should read like actions, not spreadsheets.
2. Emphasize readiness and momentum: each screen should make the next decision obvious.
3. Use bold hierarchy, not clutter: strong scale contrast and fewer competing surfaces.
4. Keep typography highly legible: readable body copy, tabular numbers, clear labels.
5. Design mobile-first without hiding power: quick paths for repeat riders, clarity for first-run riders.
